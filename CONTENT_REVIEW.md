## Notes for Reviewers

1. **Direct quotations** (items 5, 6, 18): These are the highest priority. Any passage presented in quotation marks must match the original source word-for-word.

2. **Numerical claims** (items 1, 3, 4, 7): Specific numbers lend authority but are easy to misremember. Each should be checked against the primary source.

3. **Dating claims** (items 2, 13, 15, 20, 21, 24, 26): All dates should include "c." (circa) where appropriate and note scholarly disagreement where it exists.

4. **Absence claims** (items 12, 14): Claims that something is absent from a text are particularly important to verify, as they require exhaustive search of the relevant text.

5. **Characterization of scholarly positions** (items 10, 16, 19, 22, 28): Ensure that individual scholars' views are accurately represented and not conflated with broader consensus.
